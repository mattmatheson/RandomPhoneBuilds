How to post it without losing quality

The video was professionally shot and color graded. Follow these steps so it looks that way online.

Step 0 — Save the file from Google Drive

Download the original file before you post it anywhere. Don't screen record it, don't screenshot it, and don't share it straight from the Drive app.

On your iPhone

  1. Open the Google Drive app
  2. Tap the three dots next to the video
  3. Tap DownloadWait until it's fully saved to your Photos app.
  4. Post from your Camera RollOpen Instagram and pick the downloaded file.

On a computer

  1. Go to drive.google.com
  2. Click the three dots next to the video
  3. Click DownloadIt saves to your Downloads folder.
  4. Upload that file to FacebookPick it straight from your Downloads folder.
Important
  • Don't screen record the video.
  • Don't screenshot a frame and post the screenshot.
  • Don't share straight from the Google Drive app. Always download it first.

Instagram Post from an iPhone

  1. One-time setup: turn on high-quality uploadsOpen Instagram, tap your profile photo, then tap the three lines (≡) at the top right. Go to Settings and privacy, search “Data usage,” and turn on Upload at Highest Quality. You only need to do this once. Leave it on.
  2. Connect to Wi-FiGet on Wi-Fi before you do anything else. On cellular, Instagram can compress the video even with high quality turned on.
  3. Post from your Camera RollTap +, then pick the video you downloaded from Google Drive. It should be at the top of your Camera Roll.
  4. Don't add filters or edits. Post it as-is.Tap Next without touching any filters or sliders. The video is already color graded.
  5. Post it as a Reel, then share it to your StoryChoose Reel, not a regular video post, because Reels reach a lot more people. Once it's posted, tap “Share to Story” so your followers see it too.
Never do these
  • Don't post to Instagram from an Android phone or a computer. Always use an iPhone.
  • Don't share straight from Google Drive. Always save it to your Camera Roll first.
  • Don't add Instagram filters. The video is already color graded.

Facebook Post from a computer

  1. Use a computer, not your phoneOpen facebook.com in a browser on your laptop or desktop. Facebook's desktop uploader handles big video files much better than the phone app does.
  2. Download the file from Google Drive firstFollow Step 0 above to save it to your Downloads folder before you upload anything.
  3. Upload the video file itself, not a linkCreate a new post, click Photo/Video, and pick the file you downloaded. Facebook shows fewer people posts that link to YouTube or other outside sites.
  4. Pick a thumbnail before you publishAfter the upload, Facebook lets you choose the still image people see before they press play. Pick a clear, sharp frame instead of whatever Facebook chose.
  5. If you run an ad, turn off Advantage+ CreativeIn Ads Manager, at the ad level, find the Advantage+ Creative section. Turn off “Adjust brightness and contrast” and everything else in that section. Facebook turns it back on for every new ad, and it changes the colors of the graded video. You have to turn it off by hand every time.
Shortcut if your Facebook and Instagram are linked

If quality isn't your top priority and you want one connected post that shares comments and analytics across both apps, post to Instagram first using the steps above. Once it's live, tap the three dots (···) on the post and choose Share to Facebook. If you want the best quality on Facebook, post there separately from a computer instead.

Never do these on Facebook
  • Don't post a YouTube link or a Google Drive link. Upload the file itself.
  • Don't post from your phone if quality matters. Use a computer for Facebook.
  • Don't leave Advantage+ Creative on when you run ads. It changes the colors.
