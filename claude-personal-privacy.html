Claude Personal — Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

"Claude Personal" is a private tool used only by Matt Matheson to move and organize his own files between his own computer and his own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

When Matt signs in, the tool can read and write files in the Google Drive (and other Google services he approves) of the account that signed in. It only works on files Matt chooses to upload, download or organize.

What it stores

The sign-in token is saved only on Matt's own computer. No Google data is sent to, stored by, or shared with anyone else, and nothing is sold or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.
