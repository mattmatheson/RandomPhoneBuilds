Claude Personal – Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

"Claude Personal" is a private Google API client used only by Matt Matheson's own personal assistant software, running on his own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses

With the account owner's consent, it reads and writes data in his own Google account (for example Google Sheets and Google Drive files he owns) to keep his personal notes and catalogs up to date.

How data is handled

Data is processed locally on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Access tokens are stored only on that computer and can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party access.
